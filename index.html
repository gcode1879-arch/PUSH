How to play

  • 2 to 4 players share one arena and one ball. Mix people and AI. The map is picked at random.
  • Push the ball into YOUR OWN color goal to score. If you push it into someone else's goal, THEY get the point!
  • Where you stand is how you aim. Get on the opposite side of the ball from where you want it to go.
  • Hold a direction while you push to lunge your body that way. A dotted line shows where you will go.
  • Hold PUSH to charge, then let go inside the green zone for a PERFECT push. Too early is weak. Too late fizzles.
  • First to reach the goal target wins. Trailing badly? Your next goal can count double. When someone is one goal from winning, the chasers get a faster PUSH.
  • Game modes (optional): a random silly mode lasts the whole match. Every 20 to 30 seconds a CHAOS MOMENT happens, like a super fast ball, ice or surprise bumpers.
